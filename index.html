ZH Workforce Innovation - Data & Account Deletion

App: ZH Workforce Innovation

Developer: Enas Ibrahim

1) Delete account and all related data

Users can delete their account directly from the app. This will permanently remove the account and all associated data.

  • Open the ZH Workforce Innovation app
  • From the bottom navigation bar, select "Account"
  • Tap "Delete Account Permanently"
  • Confirm deletion

This includes profile information, login data, and all associated content. Some data may be retained for up to 90 days for legal or security reasons.

2) Delete specific user data (including social links)

Users can also manage and delete specific personal data inside the app without deleting their account. This includes:

  • Social media links
  • Profile information
  • Any saved or editable user data

To do this, users can edit or remove their data directly from the profile or settings section inside the app.
